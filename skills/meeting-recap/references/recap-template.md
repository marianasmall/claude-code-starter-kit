# Recap template

Eight sections, in this order, headings exact. Drop a section only by writing "None" under it; never silently.

1. **TL;DR** — three sentences: what the meeting was about, what was decided or produced, what happens next.
2. **Decisions** — one line each: who decided, what, and any condition. "None — discussion only" if none.
3. **Action items** — `[Owner] [Action] [Due date or "no date given"]`. Owners are people, never "someone" or "the team". List the requesting user's items separately from everyone else's.
4. **Open questions** — raised, not resolved, with who owns getting the answer.
5. **Risks and concerns** — who raised it, what it threatens (timeline, money, relationship, compliance).
6. **Quotes** — up to five verbatim lines that carry someone's actual framing. Mark `(paraphrase)` if the source is cleaned text or the speaker label is uncertain.
7. **Follow-up triggers** — dated nudges: "They said Q1 launch → check in mid-December."
8. **Source** — tool and meeting ID, date, duration, attendee list as the tool reports it.

Email form: section 1 as the opening paragraph, sections 2–3 as short bullet lists, 4–7 only if non-empty, section 8 as a one-line footer. Light formatting (headers and bullets) by default, or whatever email style the user has already asked for.
